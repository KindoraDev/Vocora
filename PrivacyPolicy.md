# Vocora Privacy Policy

> Effective Date: October 15, 2025  
> Last Updated: October 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Vocora (“we”, “our”, “the App”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Service.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is essential to deliver core features:

- **User-provided content** such as story prompts, educational goals, and voice samples you upload or record.
- **Generated output** including story text, speech synthesis, and audio files returned by SiliconFlow API, as well as audio readings produced locally on your device.
- **Subscription and transaction data** (StoreKit product identifiers, receipts, and subscription status). We never store payment card information.
- **Device and diagnostic information** that helps maintain stability (e.g., crash logs or performance metrics when enabled). We do not access contacts, photos, precise location, or other sensitive personal data.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

1. Deliver story generation, voice cloning, audio creation, and playback features.  
2. Maintain subscription status, quota limits, and restore purchases.  
3. Store your stories, voices, and audio history locally for future access.  
4. Improve stability, troubleshoot issues, and enhance performance.  
5. Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

---

## 3. Storage and Security

- **Local storage**: Stories, audio files, and voice samples are stored within the app sandbox (Core Data and the file system). Removing a record or uninstalling the App deletes the associated data from the device.  
- **Network transmission**: All traffic to SiliconFlow API and Apple services uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. API keys are secured with Apple Keychain.  
- **Retention**: We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the Service, unless you delete it or withdraw consent.  
- **Protective measures**: Access controls, encrypted storage, and least-privilege practices help prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

---

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your information. Data may be shared only in these scenarios:

1. **SiliconFlow API** for processing story generation, voice cloning, or text-to-speech requests.  
2. **Apple StoreKit** for in-app purchases, subscription validation, and restore transactions.  
3. **Legal compliance** if required by law, court order, or government authority.  
4. **Business transfers** (e.g., merger or acquisition) with prior notice and continued protection of your rights.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Vocora is designed for families and children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have unintentionally collected such data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

---

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

- View, edit, or delete stories, voice samples, and audio recordings directly within the App.  
- Request export or removal of other data via the contact channel below.  
- Revoke microphone, notification, or other permissions in device settings, or uninstall the App to withdraw consent.  
- Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple App Store account.

---

## 7. International Transfers

Our partners (such as SiliconFlow) may operate in other countries. By using the App, you consent to cross-border processing where permitted by law. We take reasonable steps to protect data during such transfers.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. Significant updates will be communicated in-app or via email before they take effect.

---

## 9. Contact Us

For questions, feedback, or data requests, email us at `crossborder_lures@outlook.com`. If you require physical correspondence, please contact us by email first.
