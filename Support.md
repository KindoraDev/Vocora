## Immediate Self-Help

1. **Restore a subscription on a new device**  
   - Vocora → Settings → “Manage Subscription” → “Restore Purchases”  
   - Ensure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.  

2. **Check or upgrade your quota**  
   - Vocora → Settings → “Manage Subscription” → view current tier, quota limits, and upgrade options.  
   - If audio or story generation stops, verify that your quota has not been depleted.  

3. **Troubleshoot story/audio generation**  
   - Confirm network connectivity and retry.  
   - If the issue persists, send us the time of occurrence and any on-screen error messages so we can review server logs.  

4. **Delete saved stories or audio**  
   - Vocora → Story Library → select a story → tap the options (⋯) → “Delete Story”.  
   - Associated audio narrations are removed automatically to free storage.  

5. **Parental gate reminders**  
   - Any action that opens the App Store or an external website (privacy policy, terms of use, support page) requires a math challenge.  
   - This design protects children in accordance with Apple’s Kids Category guidelines.

## Manage Your Data

- **Request data deletion**  
  Email us with the subject line “Data Removal Request”. Provide your device identifier (Settings → About → Name) and the email address used within Vocora (if any). We will confirm and assist with deleting remaining local artifacts.  

- **Access or export local content**  
  Stories and audio files are stored locally on your device. Before uninstalling Vocora, export any stories you wish to keep by copying their text or audio via Files app.  

- **Privacy policy and terms**  
  - Privacy Policy: <https://github.com/KindoraDev/Vocora/blob/main/PrivacyPolicy.md>  
  - Terms of Use (Apple Standard EULA): <https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/>
